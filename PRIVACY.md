# Reader for X — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Reader for X does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension reads the layout of x.com / twitter.com pages in your browser only to restyle them. Nothing it reads leaves your device.
- **No network requests.** The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, tracking or third-party code.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (mode toggles, widths, appearance options) are saved with Chrome's `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome may sync them across browsers where you are signed in to Chrome. They are never sent to the developer.
- **Permissions.** The only permission is `storage`, used for the settings above. The extension runs only on `https://x.com/*` and `https://twitter.com/*`.

Reader for X is an independent project. Not affiliated with X Corp.
